I am a Teaching Professor at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI), where I teach and develop undergraduate and graduate courses in industrial and systems engineering, human factors, simulation, supply chains, design of experiments, and capstone design.

My research focuses on human factors in healthcare and transportation, with an emphasis on behavioral assessment, workload, stress, burnout, eye tracking, wearable sensing, and data-driven models of human performance. I have studied clinician workload and physiological stress in intensive care settings, tele-critical care and communication systems, and gaze-based training interventions for pilots and drivers.

Before joining RPI, I was a postdoctoral fellow at Houston Methodist and completed my PhD in Engineering Management at Western New England University. I also bring industry experience in project management, engineering design, and simulation-informed decision support.
